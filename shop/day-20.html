Day 20 · Sat 17 Oct · Copenhagen — rest day

Shop & souvenirs

Last-minute Copenhagen for design, groceries and gifts before the long flight home.

💰 Best value today

Fields Outlet Ørestad

Five minutes on the M2 from Copenhagen Towers — 60+ outlet stores across three floors (Adidas, Levi's, Björn Borg, Gant, Tommy, Superdry, Guess) at 30–50% off Danish retail. Open till 20:00 Saturday. Perfect rest-day slot: metro in, coffee, a real look at prices, back in an hour. This is where you actually buy on the whole trip.

🏷️ Stickers today · locally made

Louisiana Museum + Design Museum Denmark

👧 For her

Louisiana Museum shop (if you take the detour) — curated sticker sets keyed to current exhibitions: Kusama polka dots, Giacometti silhouettes, Hockney iPad drawings. 30–50 DKK. Only during the run.

📷 For you

Design Museum Denmark shop on Bredgade — stickers licensed from Danish design-house archives (Klint, Jacobsen, Wegner chair silhouettes). For people who care exactly which chair. Alternative: revisit Stilleben if you missed it on Day 2.

Illums Bolighus + Hay House

Must

Second look. If you spotted something on Day 2, buy it now — the airport branch has half the range.

Spice Mart · Vesterbrogade 93

Must

Restock spices for Melbourne — cheaper than any Melbourne Indian grocer and the October produce (fresh curry leaves, methi, chillies) is at peak. Fills a checked bag comfortably.

Skagen watches

Worth it

Danish minimalist watch brand since 1989. Cheaper here than Melbourne, wider selection. From 150 €.

Ecco shoes

Worth it

Danish shoe brand — quality leather, well-made walking shoes. Cheaper here than Melbourne, wider selection.

Pandora jewellery flagship, Strøget

Worth it

Danish (since 1982). Biggest global range at their Copenhagen store — including limited Copenhagen editions not sold elsewhere.

Lakrids by Bülow

Worth it

Modern Danish chocolate-covered liquorice. Small tins ~50 DKK. Distinctive Danish flavour; airport-friendly.

Bornholm smoked herring in tins

If time

Traditional Danish preserved fish from Bornholm island. Small tins 25 DKK. Keeps years.

Andersen & Maillard, Nørrebro

Worth it

The cube-croissant people. Bake fresh, pack cold, eat within 48 h. A pack of six survives the flight in cabin bag.

Coffee Collective beans (100 g)

Worth it

One last bag of freshly-roasted single-origin. Vacuum-sealed keeps 6 weeks.

Lego Store Copenhagen Airport (Kastrup)

Must

World's biggest airport Lego shop. Danish-exclusive Nyhavn / Little Mermaid / Copenhagen sets that aren't sold outside Denmark. Buy after security to include duty-free savings.

Aalborg Aquavit — Kastrup duty-free

If time

Last-chance duty-free — bottle ~200 DKK. Iconic Danish caraway spirit for the flight home.

For the little one

For the little one · Duplo Copenhagen-exclusive set

Must

World's biggest airport Lego shop at Kastrup — Nyhavn / Little Mermaid / Danish-flag Duplo sets only sold in Denmark. 200 – 800 DKK. Buy at duty-free after security.

For the little one · Danish children's books at Politikens

Worth it

Little Ida (Egon Mathiesen), Cirkeline (Hanne Hastrup), Kaj & Andrea classics. Politikens Boghandel by Rådhuspladsen. Some have English translations. From 100 DKK.

For the little one · Nyhavn colouring book

Worth it

Danish landmarks colouring book — Nyhavn, Little Mermaid, Tivoli — great in-flight activity. 60 DKK.
