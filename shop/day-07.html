Day 7 · Sun 4 Oct · Aurora day — Polar Park or Abisko

Shop & souvenirs

The shopping today is either what you missed at Polar Park yesterday or what the Aurora Sky Station shop stocks that nobody else does. Small day. Save the shopping ambition for Oslo tomorrow evening.

💰 Best value today

Bardu / Abisko have no outlet or discount store — captive-market pricing on everything. STF Abisko's shop and the Bardu Coop are your only options; both small and priced accordingly. Cook with what you carried from Setermoen yesterday.

🏷️ Stickers today · locally made

Aurora — Chad Blakley

👧 The one that means something
  • Aurora Sky Station · Chad Blakley photograph sheet — six of his aurora photos on one sheet, printed at Kiruna Reklamtryck. She saw the sky from that summit tonight. 60 SEK
💻 Laptop / helmet

Chad Blakley single-photo die-cut (Aurora Sky Station) — printed on 3M weather vinyl for aurora-photographer gear. The one for the tripod case or the L-bracket flap.

STF Abisko Turiststation shop

Must

The only real gift shop in Abisko village and it's actually good — Chad Blakley aurora prints, STF-branded wool hats and enamel mugs, the KungsledenΩrail books, and a small selection of Sámi duodji (traditional handicraft): reindeer-antler carvings, birchbark boxes, felted mittens. Everything above 200 SEK is worth it; below that is generic.

Aurora Sky Station shop (at the chairlift top)

Must

Two-room hut at 900 m: Chad Blakley photograph stickers and small prints, an aurora-cloud-map chart, hand-warmers (~40 SEK — worth it), and a laminated aurora-forecast card that translates the KP index into what you'll actually see. Only accessible with a chairlift ticket.

ICEHOTEL Jukkasjärvi shop

Worth it

Thirty minutes east across the border into Sweden proper. The hotel's shop stocks a yearly-edition sticker set by that season's ice artists (signed, dated), Sámi-inspired jewellery, and Björk-designed hoodies from a collaboration series. If you're pushing east for the aurora anyway, it's zero extra effort.

Kiruna Cafferostri (if in Kiruna)

If time

Kiruna's own coffee roaster — a bag of the "Malmberget" dark roast (~120 SEK) tastes like where you drove today: high, cold and slightly severe. Sold in the Kiruna town centre and at the STF Abisko on-and-off. Coffee kit for the fjord kitchenette tomorrow week.

Coop Konsum Björkliden

If time

Small supermarket in the ski village 10 min east of Abisko, useful if you need bread, chocolate or a bottle of Swedish Ramlösa water for the aurora night. Prices about 20% below the STF's own café.

For the little one

For the little one · Aurora Sky Station hand-warmer + patch set

Must

A small STF-branded canvas pouch with two reusable hand-warmers and three iron-on patches (aurora arc, reindeer, mountain) for ~200 SEK at the summit shop. She uses the hand-warmers on the chair on the way down and takes the patches home for the backpack.

For the little one · Sámi bell (duodji) — a real one

Worth it

The STF Abisko shop stocks small hand-turned wooden reindeer-bells made by a Sámi maker in Jokkmokk (~150 SEK). Each one a slightly different tone — the same way the herders tell their reindeer apart in the fog. Small, indestructible, tells a story.
