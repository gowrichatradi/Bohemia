Day 7 · Sun 4 Oct · Aurora day — Polar Park or Abisko

Shop & souvenirs

Henningsvær is the best browsing in Lofoten — three real workshops within two minutes of each other.

💰 Best value today

Bardu / Abisko have no outlet or discount store — captive-market pricing on everything. STF Abisko's shop and the Bardu Coop are your only options; both small and priced accordingly. Cook with what you carried from Setermoen yesterday.

🏷️ Stickers today · locally made

Aurora Sky Station + Icehotel Jukkasjärvi

👧 For her

Aurora Sky Station at 900 m — a photograph-sticker series by Chad Blakley of Lights Over Lapland, the Abisko-resident aurora photographer whose images run in National Geographic. Sold only at the chairlift top, printed at Kiruna Reklamtryck.

📷 For you

Icehotel gift shop, Jukkasjärvi (30 min east if you push) — an annual sticker series by the international artists who carve that year's hotel. A new edition every winter, dated and signed.

Engelskmannsbrygga, Henningsvær

Must

Working ceramics and glass studio on the quay — watch them throw and blow, then buy from the bench. Mugs 300 NOK, bowls 800. Unique because it's a working studio, not a shop selling imported wares.

KaviarFactory art shop

Worth it

Small prints and objects from the gallery's international artists, in a former cod-roe factory. Genuinely unusual souvenirs; nothing else like it on this trip.

Trevarefabrikken shop

Worth it

Beeswax candles, ceramics, Norwegian wool textiles. In the old woodworks — attached to the café so easy to combine.

Lofoten stockfish tin

If time

Small vacuum-sealed pouch of dried cod ("bacalao") sold at Henningsvær grocery. Same customs caveat: eat before Australia.

Lofotcaviar (cod-roe tube)

If time

Norwegian breakfast staple — smoked cod-roe caviar in a tube (like toothpaste), eaten on rye bread. 80 NOK. Weird but iconic.

For the little one

For the little one · Wooden fjord-boat toy, Trevarefabrikken

Worth it

Genuine local hand-made wooden toy. 150 – 250 NOK. Floats; simple; lovely.

For the little one · Small ceramic fish, Engelskmannsbrygga

If time

Kid-sized hand-thrown ceramic fish or seal. 200 NOK. Fragile — cabin bag.
