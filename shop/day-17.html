Day 17 · Wed 14 Oct · Prague — the day the third night bought

Shop & souvenirs

Prague day 2 · your Hestra gloves stop plus outdoor gear at Czech prices.

⭐ HESTRA gloves — Hudy Sport, Palác Fénix (Václavské náměstí)

Must

Your primary Hestra stop. Czech outdoor chain, arguably the cheapest place on the whole trip for Hestra. Full range including Army Leather Heli Ski (~4000 CZK), Fall Line (~2500 CZK), and the ski-touring collection. Also stocks Bergans, Norrøna and Warmpeace. Two floors on Wenceslas Square.

Alpine Pro, Wenceslas Square

Worth it

Czech outdoor brand (their own manufacture) — softshells, rain gear, kids' outdoor clothes. Cheap, well-made, family-oriented. Full jacket ~2000 CZK.

Warmpeace, various Prague outdoor shops

Worth it

Czech-designed premium outdoor kit — the sleeping bags are cult-favourite among European Alpine climbers. Same league as Rab or Feathered Friends at 60% of the price.

Jiřího z Poděbrad farmers market (Wed – Sat)

Worth it

Real Praguers' farmers market under Plečnik's Sacred Heart church, two metro stops from centre. Cheese, sausage, honey. Buy for the drive to Dresden.

Kofola

If time

Czech herbal cola invented under communism (1959) when the real thing wasn't available. Now nostalgic. Bottle 20 CZK. Fun for kids.

Pilsner Urquell 6-pack

Worth it

Original pilsner, invented 1842 in Plzeň — the beer that defined the whole "pilsner" style. Bottled at source. 150 – 200 CZK for six 500 ml bottles.

Prague ham (Pražská šunka)

If time

Traditional wet-cured smoked ham from Prague butchers. Best from Naše Maso in the Old Town. Vacuum-packed for the flight.

Czech picture books for the little one

Worth it

Little Mole (Krtek) storybooks in English or German — beloved Czech children's character since 1957. Souvenir with soul.

For the little one · Alpine Pro kids' rain suit / softshell

Must

Czech outdoor brand — kids' rain gear and softshells at 40 – 60% of Australian prices. Wenceslas Square store. Rain suit ~800 CZK; softshell 1200. Buy one size up.

For the little one · Krtek picture book in English

Worth it

At Palác Knih Luxor bookstore, Wenceslas Square — the biggest Prague bookshop has an English children's section with Krtek titles. 200 CZK per book.

For the little one · Kofola (kid-friendly)

If time

Fun to try — Czech herbal cola, less sweet than Coke, weird taste that kids either love or hate. 20 CZK. Cultural experience.
