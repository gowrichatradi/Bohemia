Day 10 · Wed 7 Oct · Bergen

Shop & souvenirs

Bergen for artisan wooden + wool + Norway's best chocolate, and your second Indian grocer of the trip.

Global Food · Vestre Strømkaien 1

Must

Best Indian grocer in central Bergen, across from Bergen Storsenter. Restock spices, dals, frozen paratha, fresh curry leaves. Right on your harbour walk.

Julehuset, Bryggen alley

Worth it

Norwegian Christmas decorations year-round — hand-painted wooden nisser (Christmas elves), straw stars, small carved angels. 100 – 500 NOK. Easy to pack.

Fjåk Chocolate, Bryggen

Must

Bergen's bean-to-bar chocolatier — single-origin, elegant packaging, ranked among Europe's best. Bar 90 NOK. Gift-worthy.

Røros Tweed wool blankets

Worth it

Traditional wool blankets from the Røros highlands (a UNESCO mining town). Timeless Nordic patterns. Sold at Bryggen craft shops. 1500 – 3000 NOK.

Oleana, Ole Bulls plass

If time

Norwegian textile brand. Scarves 2000+ NOK; heirloom cardigans 5000+.

Bergans Bergen, Sandbrogaten

If time

Norwegian outdoor brand's Bergen store. Backpacks, shells — same as XXL Oslo pricing.

Bergen fish market

Worth it

Fresh king crab or langoustines for eating today. Smoked and vacuum-packed salmon travels for a day if kept cold.

Karl Ove Knausgård at Norli, Torgallmenningen

If time

Norwegian literature in English — Knausgård's My Struggle, Jon Fosse (Nobel 2023), Roy Jacobsen. English editions cost about the same as home.

For the little one · Fjåk mini chocolate bars

Worth it

Bergen's bean-to-bar chocolate in small kid-friendly bars. 30 NOK each; great gift-bag fillers for cousins.

For the little one · Bergans kids' rain suit

Must

Perfect Bergen buy — a Norwegian kids' rain suit built for actually-Bergen rain. 500 – 800 NOK. Half Melbourne prices; will last her three years of preschool puddles.
