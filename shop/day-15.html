Day 15 · Mon 12 Oct · Hallstatt → Krumlov → Prague

Shop & souvenirs

Bad Ischl for imperial cakes, Krumlov for hand-carved marionettes and Bohemian silver.

Konditorei Zauner, Bad Ischl

Must

Zaunerstollen — the almond-and-nougat pastry invented here in 1832, still made in the same rooms. Sealed tins keep two months. 12 – 20 € per tin. Imperial confectioner to Franz Joseph.

Marionette workshops, Krumlov old town

Must

A living Czech craft — hand-carved wooden marionettes 800 – 3000 CZK. Buy from the small workshops off Latrán and Radniční streets (watch the carving), not the tourist stalls on the main square.

Krumlov Rose ceramics

Worth it

Local decorative rose pattern (Rožmberk-family rose, on the town crest). Small plates 300 CZK.

Bohemian crystal from workshops en route

If time

Cheaper and more authentic than Prague's tourist shops. Small workshops in South Bohemia along the road; look for "Sklo" signs.

For the little one · Krumlov Castle bear plush

Must

The castle has kept live brown bears in its moat since 1707 — the castle shop sells bear plushies with the Rožmberk rose. 300 CZK. Direct memory-souvenir link.

For the little one · Finger-puppet marionette

Worth it

Kid-sized alternative to a full marionette — a wooden-headed finger puppet from Krumlov old-town workshops. 200 – 500 CZK. Real craft, kid-safe.
