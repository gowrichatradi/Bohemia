Day 5 · Fri 2 Oct · Reine — the far west

Shop & souvenirs

The far-west day. Two tiny working shops most people never enter.

Nusfjord 1907 general store

Must

A preserved and still-working general store from 1907 — bought over-the-counter with a working till older than most Australian shops. Traditional Norwegian dry biscuits, hardtack, coffee substitutes, cod-liver oil, small wool items. 30 – 200 NOK per item. Uniquely time-capsule.

Café at Nusfjord — packaged goods

Worth it

The Nusfjord café shop bag has locally-made jams (blackcurrant, cloudberry), honey and cheese. Small tin 80 NOK. Genuinely made in the village.

Reine harbour tourist shops

If time

A handful of small shops at the harbour end of Reine — Lofoten-print tea towels, magnets, cormorant Christmas decorations. Nothing distinctive but useful for the "small gift" gap.

For the little one · Nusfjord children's book

Worth it

The old store keeps a small selection of Norwegian children's picture books, often with Lofoten themes (fishing, boats, trolls). 100 – 150 NOK. Genuine cultural souvenir.
