Day 16 · Tue 13 Oct · Prague — the old city

Shop & souvenirs

Prague is a serious shopping day — Cubist objects, Bohemian crystal, and Central Europe's best wooden toys.

Kubista, House of the Black Madonna

Must

The world's only shop selling licensed reproductions of Czech Cubist furniture, ceramics and glass (from a movement 1910-1914 that tried Cubism in 3D). Small cups 800 CZK; teapots 3000-15000. Nothing else on earth stocks this.

Manufaktura, Melantrichova

Must

Czech wooden toys, hand-painted marionettes, beeswax candles, natural cosmetics. ~40% below tourist-strip prices. Good for gifts and for the little one.

Bohemian crystal — Moser / Preciosa / Erpet

Must

Bohemian glass since the 14th century, world-famous. Moser is premium (200+ € for a wineglass), Preciosa is affordable (30 – 100 €), Erpet has the widest selection. 30-50% below Melbourne prices. Pack in checked bags with a lot of bubble wrap.

Czech garnet jewellery (Granát)

Worth it

Bohemian garnet is a bright pyrope variety, unique to a small area of northern Bohemia. Small silver ring with garnets 800 – 3000 CZK. The Granát Turnov cooperative shop is the trusted brand.

Becherovka

Worth it

Czech herbal liqueur since 1807, from Karlovy Vary — bittersweet cinnamon-anise notes. Bottle 250 CZK. Drunk before or after dinner.

Karlovarské oplatky (spa wafers)

Worth it

Round thin wafers filled with hazelnut, vanilla or chocolate. From Karlovy Vary spa town since the 1800s. Sold in every corner shop, sealed tins pack well. 60 – 150 CZK.

Havelské tržiště market

If time

The 1232 open-air market — puppets, magnets, wooden toys, cheap souvenirs. Bargainable; useful for kids' gift lists.

Manufaktura hemp / marigold cosmetics

If time

Czech natural-cosmetics brand — hemp body butter, marigold soap, thermal-spring salts. Elegant packaging, cheap. Good stocking-stuffer gifts.

Bata shoes flagship, Václavské náměstí

If time

Czech shoe brand since 1894, still designed here. Quality leather at prices 30% under Melbourne. Full Czech range not sold outside Europe.

For the little one · Krtek (Little Mole) plush & books, Manufaktura

Must

Beloved Czech children's character since 1957 — Zdeněk Miler's little black mole is a cultural icon like Miffy or Peppa. Small plush 200 CZK; picture books in English 200 CZK. She'll adopt him immediately.

For the little one · Manufaktura wooden train / building blocks

Worth it

Classic Czech wooden toys, natural finish, unstained wood. Small train 400 CZK; block sets 600 – 1000. Will grow with her for years.

For the little one · Small hand-painted wooden marionette

Worth it

Kid-sized wooden marionette — Manufaktura or workshops off the Old Town Square. 500 – 1500 CZK. Real strings, kid-friendly.
