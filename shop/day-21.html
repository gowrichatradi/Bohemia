Day 21 · Sun 18 Oct · Home

Shop & souvenirs

The way home — Kastrup duty-free is the last real shopping window on this trip.

Kastrup duty-free — Danish design

Must

The airside Georg Jensen concession has small silver pieces at duty-free prices, and the Illums Bolighus airside has a Nordic-design shortlist. Prices ~15 – 20% below the city stores.

Kastrup Lego shop

Must

Largest airport Lego shop in the world (~500 m² airside). Danish-exclusive Copenhagen sets not sold anywhere else. 200 – 800 DKK. Duty-free.

Anthon Berg + Toms chocolate

Worth it

Danish chocolate classics — marzipan, liquorice, chocolate-covered cherries. Airside pricing is at supermarket level and gift-boxes travel intact.

Aalborg Aquavit — duty-free

Worth it

Bottle ~180 DKK duty-free (vs 250 in the city). The Christmas Nordguld variety and the Taffel dark aquavit are the picks.

Lakrids by Bülow

Worth it

Chocolate-covered Danish liquorice — every airport shop stocks a range. Sealed tins 100 DKK.

Coffee Collective 250 g

If time

Their Terminal 2 shop sells the same freshly-roasted single-origin as the city roastery. Vacuum-sealed keeps 6 weeks.

Changi Terminal 3 (during the layover)

Worth it

Singapore-specific — kaya jam, Bengawan Solo pandan chiffon (fresh, eat within 24 h), tea. Kids' section has Singapore-airline plush.

For the little one · Kastrup Duplo exclusive

Must

Last chance for the Copenhagen Duplo sets (Nyhavn / Little Mermaid) that don't leave Denmark. 400 DKK. Buy on the way through.
