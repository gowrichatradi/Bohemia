Shop & souvenirs

Small workshops along the E10 — one-of-a-kind Lofoten crafts, not mass-produced tat.

💰 Best value today

Kiwi Reine

The only supermarket in Reine village itself, and the cheapest on Moskenesøya — Kiwi is Norway's discount grocery chain. Runs 08:00–22:00 daily. Beyond food there is nothing further west that resembles a cheap store; Å has a bakery only.

💻 Laptop / helmet

Kaja Solheim Reinebringen die-cut — printed on Norwegian outdoor vinyl (rated -40°C), sold at Reine Tourist Info. The one for the pelican camera case or the ski helmet.

Sund Smie forge

Must

Working blacksmith — Hans Gjertsen has hand-hammered iron cormorant sculptures at this anvil for 40 years. The Lofoten symbol. 400 – 2000 NOK depending on size. You watch it being made.

Bakeriet i Å

Worth it

Norway's oldest working bakery (since 1844, original wood-fired oven). Cinnamon buns for eating today; dry lefse (soft flatbread with cinnamon) packs and travels for weeks. 50 – 100 NOK.

Anita's Sjømat, Sakrisøy

Worth it

Vacuum-packed stockfish and salmon jerky. Careful — Norwegian customs allows the export, but Australian biosecurity does NOT. Eat before boarding, don't declare.

Cloudberry jam (multebær)

Must

Wild Arctic berry, only ripe in August-September. Small jar 80 – 120 NOK, sealed keeps a year. Uniquely Norwegian; nothing like it in Australia.

Norwegian wool socks (Devold / Aclima)

Worth it

The wool base layer fishermen have worn on this coast for centuries. Devold has been made in Norway since 1853. 200 – 400 NOK per pair; last decades.

Reindeer horn buttons / small carvings

If time

Sami-tradition small carvings — sold at Anita's and Å museum shops. 100 – 400 NOK, travels flat.

For the little one

For the little one · Small wooden Viking longship

Worth it

Hand-carved miniature Viking boats at Anita's Sjømat and Å museum shop. 100 – 300 NOK. Floats in the bath.

For the little one · Norwegian trollcross pendant

If time

Simple twisted-iron good-luck charm — Norwegian folk-tradition. 80 NOK, at Sund Smie or Å shops. Kid-safe and legend-friendly.
