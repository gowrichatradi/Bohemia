Day 6 · Sat 3 Oct · Reine → Polar Park (aurora chase east)

Shop & souvenirs

Two proper workshops on today's drive — heavy but unique.

💰 Best value today

Coop Prix Setermoen

Straight on your E6 line south toward Bardu — the closest supermarket to Polar Park. Fill up with lunch and dinner things before the park; the café inside is Norwegian-cafeteria priced. Coop Prix is Coop's discount format, cheapest chain of the day.

🏷️ Stickers today · locally made

Polar Park gift shop, Bardu

👧 For her

The predator lineup as one sheet — wolf, brown bear, lynx, wolverine, elk, musk ox — for ~85 NOK. Illustrated by Grafisk Nord in Tromsø from the actual individuals in the enclosures; you can walk past the sticker's subject.

📷 For you

Same shop, the Polar Park logo in oval-plate car-sticker form (45 NOK) — the vinyl that says yes I drove to Bardu in October. Sold nowhere else.

Vikten Glasshytta

Must

The only Arctic-style hand-blown glass workshop in the world (since 1974). Åsvar Tangrand developed a thick-walled Arctic glass style unlike anywhere else. Small heavy ornaments 300 – 800 NOK; larger pieces 5000+.

Lofotr Viking Museum shop

Worth it

Real hand-forged reproduction Viking jewellery. Bronze bracelets ~600 NOK, silver Thor's-hammer pendants ~800. Good gift for the little one's costume drawer.

Hand-knitted Selbu-pattern mittens

Worth it

The traditional 8-pointed star knitting pattern from Selbu (invented 1857) — distinctly Norwegian. 300 – 500 NOK. Warm and packs flat.

Village postcards from Å / Nusfjord

If time

The tiny village shops sell postcards of themselves that always run out. Buy on the spot at 15-25 NOK.

For the little one

For the little one · Lofotr Viking shield / wooden sword

Must

Real reproduction kit from the museum shop, kid-sized and safe wood. 200 – 400 NOK. Instant hit — she can wear it around the longhouse and take it home.

For the little one · Small wooden Viking horse

Worth it

Traditional carved wooden horse toy from the Viking-museum shop. 150 NOK. Simple, indestructible.
