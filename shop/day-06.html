Day 6 · Sat 3 Oct · Reine → Polar Park (aurora chase east)

Shop & souvenirs

A driving day out of Lofoten and into Bardu. Provisions before you leave the coast, wildlife-park merch at the destination, one liquor stop for the aurora night — that's the day.

💰 Best value today

Coop Prix Setermoen

Straight on your E6 line south toward Bardu — the closest supermarket to Polar Park. Fill up with lunch and dinner things before the park; the café inside is Norwegian-cafeteria priced. Coop Prix is Coop's discount format, cheapest chain of the day.

🏷️ Stickers today · locally made

Polar Park — the wolves she stood next to

👧 The one that means something
  • Polar Park predator sheet — wolf, brown bear, lynx, wolverine, elk, musk ox on one sheet. She can point at each enclosure she stood at. 85 NOK
  • Wolf-pack alpha portrait — the specific alpha female born at the park in 2019, drawn by keeper Silje Åhlen from life. 60 NOK
💻 Laptop / helmet

Polar Park oval logo (car-plate style) — 3M reflective vinyl, engineered for -30°C and pump-station car washes. The one that says yes I drove to Bardu.

Polar Park gift shop, Bardu

Must

Wolf, bear, lynx, wolverine, elk and musk-ox merch — but the honest picks are the small ones: enamel pin badges of each animal (~120 NOK), the predator-illustration tea towel (~180 NOK), and the printed field guide to the park's individual animals with keeper notes (~220 NOK). Skip the plush; every gift shop has plush.

Coop Prix Setermoen — provisions run

Worth it

Not a souvenir stop but the essential one — brunost, coffee, bread, breakfast eggs, oat milk for the aurora hotel kitchenette. Also the cheapest bottled water on the day. Open till 22:00 Saturday even in the shoulder season.

Vinmonopolet Setermoen

Worth it

The state liquor shop, and the only one for 90 km either way. If you want an aquavit for the aurora night — the traditional Linie is aged across the equator on a ship, which is a fact worth telling the room. ~350 NOK a bottle. Closes 15:00 sharp on Saturdays.

Bardu Bygdetun folk museum shop

If time

Tiny folk-museum shop with hand-knitted mittens, Sámi-inspired ceramics and small books on the Bardu valley's twin Norwegian/Sámi history. Everything ~30% below Oslo museum prices. Open Sat 12:00–16:00 in the shoulder season; ring first (+47 77 18 51 45).

Nusfjord 1907 general store — on the way out

If time

If you swing through Nusfjord on the drive east out of Reine, the preserved 1907 general store still sells honest goods (bread, salt cod, dried fruit, honey, wool socks) at not-tourist prices, alongside the small souvenir line. The building alone is worth ten minutes — restored to how it looked when the last fisherman bought his winter tobacco here.

For the little one

For the little one · Polar Park predator badge set

Must

Six small enamel pin badges of the park's predators, sold as a set at the gift shop for ~350 NOK. Small enough for the backpack strap, tough enough to survive her handling. Better than the plush; she'll remember which enclosure she stood at for each one.

For the little one · Coop Prix "Barnas Turpakke"

Worth it

Norwegian supermarket kids' hike-day pack — chocolate raisins, a Yoggi yoghurt drink, a small pack of Sørlandschips, all in a paper bag with a colouring sheet on the back. ~80 NOK. The colouring sheet is a wolf and a moose. Perfectly on-day.
