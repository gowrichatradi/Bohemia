Day 13 · Sat 10 Oct · Bergen → Munich → Neuschwanstein

Shop & souvenirs

Bavaria has your backup for outdoor gear if you don't want to wait for Prague — plus proper Alpine crafts.

Sport Conrad, Garmisch-Partenkirchen

Worth it

Regional heavyweight since 1934 — family-run, three stores in Garmisch. Full outdoor range (Ortovox, Arc'teryx, Mammut, Hestra). Prices ~10-15% below Norway. But your Hestra target is Prague — save this trip for other kit.

Wallgau & Mittenwald village shops

Must

Traditional Bavarian crafts. Lüftlmalerei prints (the pastel painted-facade art), hand-carved wooden nutcrackers, real dairy cowbells 100 – 400 €. Better prices than Munich souvenir stalls.

Mittenwald violin makers

If time

This town has made violins since 1684 (Matthias Klotz trained in Cremona under Amati). Beginner student violins 300 – 800 €; handmade 3000+. Only meaningful if you play.

Nymphenburg porcelain, Munich

If time

Bavaria's oldest porcelain manufactory (since 1747), Munich flagship near the palace. Meissen's rival. Small figurine 300+ €.

Real dairy cowbell

Worth it

Genuine working cowbells from local Alpine dairies (not the mass-produced souvenirs). Every village dairy sells them. 60 – 300 € depending on size and craftsmanship.

Loden coat

If time

Traditional Bavarian boiled-wool coat, waterproof, worn since the 1500s. Serious buy at 200 – 600 €; wear for a lifetime.

For the little one · Steiff teddy bear

Must

THE original teddy bear, made in Germany since 1880 (the "teddy" name was actually coined by Steiff). Small plush 30 – 60 €. Sold at Sport Conrad Garmisch and every serious toy shop. A lifelong heirloom.

For the little one · Playmobil small set

Worth it

Germany-based since 1974. Kids' castle / knight sets that fit the Neuschwanstein trip. Small blister packs 5 – 15 €; boxed sets 20 – 80 €.

For the little one · Ravensburger puzzle

Worth it

German puzzle-maker since 1883. Toddler puzzles at every supermarket. From 5 €. Rainy-day plane-ride essential.

For the little one · Kids' dirndl or lederhosen

If time

Adorable in miniature. Trachten shop in Mittenwald or Wallgau. 40 – 150 € for a proper set; wearable dress-up for the next two years.

🏍️ Louis Motorrad, Munich / Garmisch

Must

Germany's biggest motorcycle-apparel chain — the place to buy on this trip. Full range of Rev'it, Alpinestars, Held, Dainese, Sidi. Their own Louis-brand gear is quality-competitive at half the price. Prices 30 – 40% below Melbourne. Munich has three big stores.

🏍️ Polo Motorrad, Munich

Must

Louis's main rival — often slightly better deals on the same brands. Munich has 5+ locations.

🏍️ Ducati Munich dealership

Must

Full Ducati Scrambler apparel line, Icon 1100 branded accessories. Scrambler jacket ~€400 – 500; helmets 300 – 800.

🏍️ Held gloves at Munich stockists

Must

German premium glove-maker. Widely regarded as best sport / touring glove in the world. Air Stream 3.0 ~120 €; Steve gloves ~180 €.

🏍️ Riding jeans (Rev'it Philly / Roland Sands)

Worth it

Scrambler-appropriate cafe-racer / retro riding jeans with Kevlar / Dyneema panels. At Louis Bavaria. ~180 – 280 €.
