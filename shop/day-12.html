Day 12 · Fri 9 Oct · Aurlandsfjord → Hardanger → the airport side

Shop & souvenirs

Hardanger cider country in harvest week — the strongest single food-buying day of the trip.

Syse Gård cider, Ulvik

Must

Family-run cidery (since the 1720s) — Hardanger cider is PDO-protected like champagne, only apples from this region can bear the name. Still and sparkling. 200 NOK per bottle. Check bags — 750 ml doesn't fly cabin.

Ulvik Frukt & Cideri

Worth it

Another family producer, tasting room. Slightly different style — try both.

Hardanger Saft- og Siderfabrikk

Worth it

Presses juice as well as cider — apple, plum, blackcurrant. The kids' answer to the tasting rounds.

Hardanger embroidery (Hardangersøm)

Worth it

Traditional Norwegian whitework — geometric patterns on linen, cottage-industry since the 1600s. Small runners 400 NOK, tablecloths 1500+. Distinctive Norwegian textile art.

Ulvik apples (fresh eating)

If time

Peak season for Hardanger Aroma and Gravenstein apples — sweeter and firmer than Australian supermarket. Eat over the drive; you can't take fruit back.

For the little one · Apple-picking bag at the cider farm

Worth it

Small child's apple-picking bag at Syse Gård — free with a cider tasting, or 50 NOK. Souvenir + immediate activity.

For the little one · Norwegian folk-tale illustrated book

If time

Asbjørnsen & Moe Norwegian fairy tales in English (illustrated by Theodor Kittelsen). At Ulvik village bookshop. 150 NOK. Trolls, three billy goats, forest-dark and beautiful.
