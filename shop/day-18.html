Day 18 · Thu 15 Oct · Prague morning → Dresden

Shop & souvenirs

Two very serious German craft traditions within a short drive.

💰 Best value today

Elbepark Dresden — on the D8 arrival

Northwest edge of Dresden right on the A4 you come in on — Elbepark is Saxony's largest shopping centre (170 stores: Primark, C&A, TK Maxx, Deichmann). Adds 15 min to the drive from Prague. Fifteen-minute stroll for a warm jumper, or an hour for a proper look — the arrival window bought by the breakfast start is exactly this.

🏷️ Stickers today · locally made

Kunsthofpassage singing façade + Meissen crossed swords

👧 For her

Kunsthofpassage Hof der Elemente courtyard sells a hand-printed sticker of the singing funnel-and-gutter façade (~€4). Designed by Annette Paul, Christoph Roßner and André Tempel, the artists who built the wall in 1999. Only there.

📷 For you

Meissen factory outlet — the historic crossed-swords logo in sticker form (~€2). The porcelain mark used continuously since 1723, pressed at the factory itself.

Meissen Porcelain flagship, Dresden Altstadt

Must

Europe's oldest porcelain manufactory (since 1710) — Meissen invented European porcelain. Tea cup from 200 €; small figurines 500 – 3000 €. The actual factory is 30 min north-west in Meissen town if you want the outlet with 40% off seconds.

Erzgebirge wooden crafts, Neumarkt shops

Must

Nutcrackers, incense-smokers (Räuchermann) and Christmas pyramids from the Ore Mountains south of Dresden — carved by families here for 400 years, still by hand. 30 – 300 €.

Wendt & Kühn wooden angels

Must

Signature hand-painted 11-dot polka-dot wooden angel figurines, made in Grünhainichen (Erzgebirge) since 1915. Every German family has one. Small figure 60 – 150 €; large 300+.

Dresdner Christstollen

Worth it

The original Christmas cake — a Dresden-protected recipe (since 1474) with a legal definition of what "Dresdner Stollen" means. Sealed loaves keep 6 months. Every Altstadt bakery.

Radeberger Pilsner

Worth it

Local Saxon brewery since 1872. 6-pack ~10 €.

Saxon Sekt (sparkling wine)

If time

From the Elbe valley vineyards north of Dresden. Bottled at Radebeul. Check bags.

For the little one

For the little one · Steiff teddy bear

Worth it

If you didn't buy one in Bavaria — every serious Dresden toy shop stocks Steiff. 30 – 100 € for small classics.

For the little one · Erzgebirge small nutcracker

Worth it

Kid-sized versions of the classic soldier nutcracker, from the Ore Mountains south of Dresden. 15 – 40 €. Traditional carved wood, painted uniforms.

For the little one · Ravensburger puzzle

If time

German puzzle flagship. Toy stores off Neumarkt. From 5 €.

For the little one · Wendt & Kühn small angel

If time

A single hand-painted 11-dot angel for the child's shelf. 60 €. A German christening-gift tradition.

🏍️ Louis Motorrad, Dresden

Worth it

Same chain as Munich — smaller store, same prices.

🏍️ Kradwerk Dresden

If time

Vintage-motorcycle specialty shop — original 1970s BMW and Honda parts, custom cafe-racer / scrambler build parts, retro helmets. Very Scrambler-scene.
