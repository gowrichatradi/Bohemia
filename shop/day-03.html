Day 3 · Wed 30 Sep · Denmark → Reine

Shop & souvenirs

A transit day with two duty-free windows and one useful supermarket.

💰 Best value today

Coop Extra Leknes

The last real supermarket for 90 km — an hour short of Reine on the E10. Norwegian grocery prices are already brutal, and Coop Extra is the cheapest chain by a clear margin. Stock the Airbnb kitchen here: bread, brunost, coffee, oat milk, breakfast eggs. Everything from Reine onwards costs 25–40% more.

🏷️ Stickers today

Flight day. Kastrup and Evenes gift kiosks stock mass-market souvenir vinyl — skip. Save it for the Reine Tourist Info tomorrow, where Kaja Solheim's hand-drawn prints are.

Kastrup duty-free — real shopping window

Must

You're airside for ~90 min before SK2494 to Evenes. The duty-free hall at Kastrup is one of the largest in Europe. Grab Danish chocolates (Anthon Berg, Toms), Aalborg Aquavit for the rorbu kitchen, and (if you want) a Lego set at the world's biggest airport Lego shop.

Evenes airport shop

If time

Tiny arrivals shop at Harstad-Narvik. Norwegian-brand wool (Marius socks, Devold hats), Freia chocolate, Sami-inspired keychains. Overpriced but the only stop between the plane and 4h30 of driving.

Coop Extra Leknes (an hour short of Reine)

Must

Full-size supermarket — the last one before Reine. Beyond food, it also stocks cheap wool socks, kids' colouring books, Norwegian children's snacks (KIMS, Freia), fishing lures for the harbour, batteries and camera-shop basics. 15 min stop.

Svolvær harbour — quick café stop

If time

Halfway break — the marina cafés also sell postcards, cormorant-motif tea towels, and Lofoten-branded souvenirs. Nothing you couldn't get later but the walk is the real reason.

For the little one

For the little one · Norwegian wool socks at Coop

Worth it

Cheap kid-size wool socks at Coop for the rorbu evenings — Devold or Coop's own brand. 100 – 150 NOK. Warmer than anything from home.
