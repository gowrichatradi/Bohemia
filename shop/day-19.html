Day 19 · Fri 16 Oct · Dresden, all day → Berlin airport

Shop & souvenirs

The last mainland day — Dresden morning, then a drive to Berlin BER. Two proper windows.

Dresden Neumarkt shops — last-chance

Must

Last chance for Meissen porcelain, Erzgebirge nutcrackers, Wendt & Kühn angels, and Dresdner Stollen at source. If you skipped Day 18 shopping, do it this morning.

Radeberger, Landskron or Feldschlößchen beer

Worth it

Local Saxon breweries. A 6-pack at any supermarket in Dresden or on the road to Berlin — ~10 €. Bottles carry the local labels not sold outside Germany.

Sachsen wine — Radebeul

If time

Twenty minutes north-west of Dresden along the Elbe. One of Europe's northernmost wine regions, dry Rieslings and Weißburgunder. Buy at Winzergenossenschaft Meissen. From 12 € a bottle. Check bags.

Berlin BER airport — German duty-free

Worth it

After bag-drop and security. German brands cheaper than anywhere on the trip so far: Ritter Sport blocks, Haribo variety, Milka slabs, Berentzen liqueur, small bottles of Jägermeister. Fill a small bag for gifts.

🏍️ Louis Motorrad Berlin (if you have time before BER)

If time

Berlin has Louis stores you'd pass en route to BER (Wildau branch is on the A113 to the airport). Last chance for German motorcycle-apparel prices before you leave. Rev'it, Held, Alpinestars.

For the little one · Berlin BER duty-free Haribo

Worth it

Buy a mixed pack of Haribo classics — sizes and flavours not sold in Australia. 5 € each, keep well, plane-friendly.
