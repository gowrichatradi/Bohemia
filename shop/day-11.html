Day 11 · Thu 8 Oct · Bergen → Aurlandsfjord

Shop & souvenirs

Small-producer food and craft along the fjord — buy direct at prices half of what you'd see in Oslo.

Undredal Stølsysteri

Must

The only place to buy hand-made brown goat cheese (geitost / brunost) at source. Made in this village for centuries. 180 NOK a wedge; vacuum-packed travels fine. 40% cheaper than city retailers.

Ægir Bryggeri, Flåm

Worth it

The stave-church-shaped brewery sells its own bottled beer. Rallar Amber Ale and Julebrygg (Christmas ale) are the strong picks. Around 80 NOK per bottle.

Norwegian cheese slicer (osthøvel)

Must

Invented in Norway in 1925 by Thor Bjørklund — a wooden-handled tool that shaves off perfect cheese slices. Universal in Nordic kitchens. 100 – 200 NOK; simple, indestructible.

Undredal wool mittens

Worth it

Small hand-knit mittens and hats from local wool, made by the village women. 300 NOK. Genuine cottage-industry, not tourist manufacture.

Njord, Gudvangen

If time

Small Sogn crafts — wool, wood, hand-forged iron. Everything made within 50 km. Modest selection, honest prices.

For the little one · Undredal goat plush or wooden goat

Must

Undredal has more goats than people — the dairy shop sells small hand-made wooden goats and plushies. 100 – 200 NOK. Perfect village-specific souvenir.

For the little one · Wooden Flåm Railway train

Worth it

Model train kit from the Flåm station gift shop. 200 – 500 NOK. Ties to her day's train ride.
