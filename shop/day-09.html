Day 9 · Tue 6 Oct · The Bergensbanen → Bergen

Shop & souvenirs

A train day — but three station stops each carry a shop, and the dining car has a small shelf.

💰 Best value today

You're on the Bergensbanen 12:03 → 19:00 — no outlet on the line. Voss station has a small Kiwi if you overrun on train snacks. Save the shopping for Bergen tomorrow.

🏷️ Stickers today · locally made

Bergensbanen — the actual train

👧 The one that means something
  • Voss station kiosk — the NSB Bergensbanen scenic sticker of the train weaving through Hardangervidda snow, printed once a season. Grab it in your 8-minute halt. 35 NOK
💻 Laptop / helmet

Finse Amundsen die-cut (Wilse archive) — museum-licensed matte vinyl for camera bodies and lens cases. Only sold in the Finse station shop.

Oslo S concourse — Byporten shopping

Worth it

Before boarding: the Byporten mall attached to Oslo S has a Norrøna store, Åhlens (Nordic department store), an outdoor kids' shop, and a great Norwegian design bookshop. Twenty minutes is enough.

Finse station shop (15-min stop)

If time

1,222 m up on the plateau. The tiny station shop sells Finse-specific wool caps and mugs, Hardangerjøkulen glacier postcards, and one specific book: Fram over polhavet reprints. Overpriced but that's the whole point of being here.

Voss station bakery

Worth it

A quick jump-off on the drop from the plateau. Voss is known for smoked mutton sausage (vossakorv) — the platform bakery sells vacuum-packed rings that survive to Bergen. Also cinnamon buns for the last hour.

Bergensbanen dining car

If time

Sells postcards, a laminated route map (100 NOK — the best souvenir of a Slow-TV-famous train ride), coffee and small Freia bars.

For the little one

For the little one · Slow TV route map

Worth it

The dining-car laminated route map showing every tunnel and station is the memory-souvenir for a train-loving kid. 100 NOK. Small, waterproof, memorable.
