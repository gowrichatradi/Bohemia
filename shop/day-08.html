Day 8 · Mon 5 Oct · Bardu / Abisko → Oslo

Shop & souvenirs

Fly-in-to-Oslo day — the evening after DY365 lands is when the shopping actually happens. Central Oslo is walking distance from the Børsparken hotel; XXL Sport, Norrøna House and Freia chocolate all in a two-hour loop.

💰 Best value today

XXL Sport Oslo City

You land Oslo 17:25 with the evening open. XXL Sport at Oslo City mall is a five-minute walk from Oslo Central and from the Børsparken hotel. Deep discount racks at the back: last-season Norrøna, Bergans and Devold at 30–60% off flagship prices. If she needs a warmer base layer before the fjord leg, this is where you buy it — not the Norrøna House on Bogstadveien.

🏷️ Stickers today · locally made

Grünerløkka independents + Fri Flyt

👧 For her

The small independents around Grünerløkka — Fransk Bazar on Markveien, Norway Says pop-ups — carry hand-illustrated Oslo-tram, Munch-Scream and Vigelandpark prints. Rotates weekly; the sticker never repeats.

📷 For you

Fri Flyt magazine shop on Møllerveien — Norwegian outdoor-culture stickers (ski, climbing, kayaking) by the named illustrators behind Fri Flyt's own magazine covers. 40–90 NOK each.

XXL Sport Oslo City

Must

Deep-discount Norwegian outdoor chain — five minutes from Oslo S. Back-wall clearance racks routinely have last-season Norrøna insulation jackets at 40% off, Devold merino at 30% off, and Bergans daypacks half price. Not glamorous, honest, exactly the kit you need for the fjord leg.

Norrøna House, Bogstadveien 30

Worth it

The flagship, and worth walking through even if you buy nothing — the atrium is a two-storey climbing wall and the current-season Lofoten Gore-Tex range is beautiful. If a proper down jacket is on the list, this is where they carry every colour and size. Prices are Norwegian retail, but the staff will actually fit you.

Devold merino base layers

Worth it

Made in Langevåg near Ålesund since 1853 — the oldest continuously running Norwegian textile brand. Base layers 700–1,200 NOK at XXL clearance, 900–1,600 NOK at Norrøna House. Merino wool is what you should be wearing for the next week.

Freia Melkesjokolade

Worth it

Norway's own milk chocolate since 1889, from a factory in Grünerløkka still going. A 200 g bar is ~40 NOK at Coop, more at the airport. The mint dark version is the local pick nobody outside Norway ever finds.

Oleana textiles

If time

Colour-block Norwegian wool cardigans and jackets, made in Ytre Arna near Bergen. Not cheap (3,500 NOK a cardigan) but Norway's most recognisable modern textile brand. Small store on Grensen. Note this if you're buying serious Norwegian craft; otherwise pass.

Vippa food-hall shop

If time

Small dedicated food shop at the Vippa harbour hall — tinned Norwegian mackerel and cod-roe, Kvæfjord cake in a jar, jars of cloudberry jam from Finnmark. Better prices and better producers than any airport shop.

For the little one

For the little one · Freia mini-bar advent-style set

Must

Small paper wallet of 20 mini Freia milk-chocolate bars, ~90 NOK at any Kiwi or Coop. Ration one per day for the fjord leg and it will get her onto the Bergensbanen tomorrow. The wrapper illustrations rotate seasonally.

For the little one · XXL kids' wool beanie

Worth it

Norwegian brands (Kari Traa, Bergans, Devold) make proper 100% merino kids' beanies with patterns she'll want to keep. ~200 NOK at XXL clearance racks. She's going to lose one; buy two.

For the little one · Munch mini-book at the National Museum shop

If time

The Nationalmuseet shop stocks a "Munch for children" board-book (~150 NOK) — The Scream in cartoon form, plus the paintings she can look at when you actually visit the Munch Museum on a future trip. Open till 20:00 Mondays.
