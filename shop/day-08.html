Day 8 · Mon 5 Oct · Svolvær → Oslo

Shop & souvenirs

Oslo is your best-and-cheapest chance for Norwegian outdoor gear + Norwegian food classics.

XXL Sport & Villmark

Must

Cheapest full-range outdoor chain in Norway (Storo or Alna are the big branches). Carries Norrøna, Bergans, Devold, Fjällräven, Hestra. This is where Norwegians shop — check the sale rack.

Norrøna House, Bogstadveien 30

Worth it

Norway's premium outdoor brand's flagship. Pricier than XXL but the full range and the design pieces XXL doesn't stock.

Devold merino base layers

Must

Norwegian merino wool since 1853 — arguably the best base layer on the market. Available at Sport 1 and Skandinavisk Høyfjellsutstyr. ~500 NOK per piece; will last a decade.

Dale of Norway sweaters

Worth it

The iconic red-white-blue Marius jumper (Norwegian snowflake pattern, since 1879). Full pullover ~2500 NOK; a proper Norwegian heirloom.

Oleana textiles

If time

Norwegian silk-and-wool scarves and cardigans, made in Ytre Arna. Not cheap (2000+ NOK for a scarf) but heirloom quality.

Freia Melkesjokolade

Must

Norway's milk chocolate since 1889 — the Cadbury of Norway. 30 NOK a bar; every supermarket. Buy 10 bars for gifts.

Freia Kvikklunsj

Must

Norway's answer to Kit-Kat — every Norwegian hiker carries one. Iconic red-yellow packaging. 100 NOK a 10-pack.

Linie Aquavit

Worth it

Norwegian aquavit matured in sherry casks that literally cross the Equator twice on a ship to Australia and back before bottling. Uniquely Norwegian. 350 NOK bottle.

For the little one · Ringrose Design troll figurine

Must

The iconic ugly-cute Norwegian troll — every Norwegian child has one. Small 200 NOK, big ones 800+. From Norway Shop or Freia Torg.

For the little one · Kubb (Viking lawn game)

Worth it

Traditional Nordic wooden lawn game — throw sticks to knock over blocks. 400 NOK. Grows with the child through primary school.

For the little one · Reindeer or polar bear plush, Bygdøy museum shops

Worth it

From the Fram Museum or Folkemuseum — quality plush, not tat. 200 – 400 NOK.

For the little one · Norrøna Kids or Bergans Kids at XXL

Worth it

Norwegian kids' outdoor gear — rain suits, insulated jackets. Best quality outdoor kit for children in the world, roughly half of Melbourne prices. From 400 NOK.
