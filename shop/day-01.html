Day 1 · Mon 28 Sep · Melbourne → Copenhagen

Shop & souvenirs

First day — half day on jet-lag. One quick pass on the way in from the airport, and the airport itself on the way out later.

Kastrup duty-free (on arrival — hold for the return)

If time

Note this rather than shop it. On the way home (Day 21) the same duty-free zone is where you'll grab Aalborg Aquavit, Lakrids liquorice tins, Danish chocolates, and the last-chance Lego. Cheaper than the city shops by ~20%. Do a mental list now.

Sonny bakery, Rådhusstræde

Worth it

On your walking route from Kongens Nytorv to the palace — pick up a cardamom bun for immediate consumption, and a bag of the ground cardamom-and-cinnamon spice mix they use. 50 DKK.

Small design at Illums Bolighus (browsing only)

If time

Walk through Illums today to note what to buy tomorrow — HAY trays, Kähler ceramics, Royal Copenhagen. Don't buy yet; you've got two more Copenhagen days.

For the little one · airport Lego stand

Worth it

Kastrup arrivals has a small Lego stand — a single Nyhavn Duplo brick for 60 DKK is a good welcome-to-Denmark toy that keeps her busy at the hotel.
