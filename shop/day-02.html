Day 2 · Tue 29 Sep · Copenhagen

Shop & souvenirs

Copenhagen for take-home Danish design, coffee beans, Indian spices for the road, and the last real supermarket run before Lofoten.

Illums Bolighus, Amagertorv

Must

Danish design department store since 1925 — Georg Jensen silver, Royal Copenhagen porcelain, HAY furniture, Kähler ceramics all in one building. The souvenir sweet spot.

HAY House (upstairs Illums)

Must

HAY is the affordable young sister of Illums — colourful trays, ceramics, wool textiles, storage boxes. 200 – 800 DKK per piece. Pack in checked bag.

Royal Copenhagen — Amagertorv

Worth it

Denmark's oldest porcelain (since 1775). The hand-painted blue Flora Danica pattern is iconic. Small mug ~350 DKK, plate ~800 DKK. Second-tier factory-outlet items are 30% cheaper.

Georg Jensen

Worth it

Danish silversmith since 1904 — clean modernist jewellery and silverware. Small silver pendant from ~1500 DKK; beloved by the design world.

Spice Mart · Vesterbrogade 93

Must

Central Indian grocer — Everest and MDH spices, dals, atta, ghee, methi, fresh curry leaves. Last real chance to load up before three weeks of nothing but Nordic supermarkets.

Coffee Collective (inside Torvehallerne)

Must

The roastery that started Nordic light-roasting in 2008. Freshly-roasted single-origin beans. Vacuum-sealed 250 g bag ~140 DKK; keeps 6 weeks. World-class.

Lakrids by Bülow

Worth it

Modern Danish chocolate-covered liquorice (salted, salted caramel, chilli). Danes are obsessed. Small tins ~50 DKK; gift-worthy and airport-friendly.

Aalborg Aquavit

If time

Traditional Danish caraway-spiced spirit, distilled since 1846. Signature Christmas drink. Bottle ~200 DKK; duty-free at airport on the way home.

Lego Store, Vimmelskaftet

Worth it

Denmark invented Lego. Central store carries exclusive Nyhavn / Little Mermaid / Copenhagen sets not sold outside Denmark. Airport store is bigger; buy on the way out.

For the little one · Duplo (Lego for toddlers)

Worth it

Bigger blocks version of Lego, designed for 1.5 – 5-year-olds. Same Danish invention, safer at three. Sets 200 – 500 DKK at any Lego Store or supermarket toy aisle. Danish-exclusive Copenhagen sets only in Denmark.

For the little one · Halfdan Rasmussen picture books

Worth it

Denmark's greatest 20th-century children's poet — nonsense-rhyme illustrated books, in Danish (buy for the art) or English translation. Politikens Boghandel by Rådhuspladsen has both. From 100 DKK.

For the little one · Copenhagen Zoo polar bear plush

If time

If you did the zoo yesterday, the shop sells a signature Arctic Ring polar bear plush. 200 DKK. A trip souvenir with a specific memory attached.
