Day 14 · Sun 11 Oct · Geroldsee → the Salzkammergut

Shop & souvenirs

Salt from the world's oldest mine, and Austria's oldest working ceramics factory.

Salzwelten Hallstatt shop

Must

Rock salt straight from the world's oldest continuously-worked mine (7,000 years). Small blocks and grinders 5 – 20 €. Unique take-home from a place with a 300-generation salt-mining history.

Gmundner Keramik factory outlet, Gmunden

Must

The famous green-striped Austrian ceramics — in production since 1492. Factory-outlet prices are 30-50% below the Vienna retail store. Twenty minutes off the E-road. Small mug 15 €; teapot 60 €.

Mozartkugeln from Fürst (Salzburg)

Worth it

The original Mozart-ball chocolate, invented in Salzburg in 1890 by Paul Fürst. Widely sold. Small box 10 €. (The mass-produced red-foil Mirabell version is at every supermarket and airport.)

Hand-carved wooden nativity figures

Worth it

Alpine folk tradition — carved by mountain families through winters. Individual figures 20 – 80 €; full nativity sets 200+.

Alpine loden vest

If time

Traditional boiled-wool waistcoat. Serious Alpine wear; 100 – 250 €.

For the little one · Salzwelten Hallstatt kids' salt kit

Worth it

Small salt-crystal-growing science kit or bag of Alpine rock salt with a mini-grinder. 5 – 15 €. Souvenir + kitchen activity for later.

For the little one · Hand-carved wooden salt-miner figure

If time

Traditional Alpine tabletop-toy figure of a miner with pickaxe and headlamp. 15 – 30 €. Part of the region's folk-art tradition.
