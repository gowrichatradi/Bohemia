Day 6 · Sat 3 Oct · Reine → Polar Park (aurora chase east)

East out of Lofoten — wolves at 68°N

The E10 drops you out of the Lofoten wall and onto the mainland at Tjeldsund, then the E6 runs south through the Bardu valley. You are heading for a wildlife park the size of a small country and a night sky where the aurora has better odds than it does behind you.

Did you knowPolar Park in Bardu is the world's northernmost animal park (68°42′N). The predator enclosures are between 8 and 12 hectares each — an area a European zoo would spread its entire collection across — so the wolves you see are moving through actual forest, not pacing an artificial run.

The E10 through Lofoten

Opened 1963 · fully continuous 2007

The road you drive today did not exist as one continuous line until 1963, and even then it stopped where the ferries began. The last landlocked stretch — the fixed link to the mainland — was finished in 2007 when the Lofast tunnel opened. Before that, leaving Lofoten meant timing a ferry from Melbu; after it, you could drive out at 03:00 if the aurora went east.

Notice the fjord tunnels near Tjeldsundet — one of them drops 260 m below sea level. Norway builds these where any other country would build a bridge, because bridges freeze in winter and tunnels don't.

Bardu & the Sámi valley

Settled 1791 by families from Østerdalen · Sámi presence >1000 years

Bardu is unusual: it was settled in one wave in the 1790s by families the Danish-Norwegian crown moved north from Østerdalen (450 km south) to farm valleys the Sámi had used for reindeer pasture. The two communities have shared the valley ever since — the modern town has a Norwegian church and a Sámi day school, both on the same street. The Bardu Bygdetun folk museum has one room of each.

Notice the E6 signage in dual language: Norwegian on top, Northern Sámi below (Bárdu). This is the southern edge of the official Sámi administrative area.

Polar Park, Bardu

Opened 1994 · enclosures 8–12 hectares each · 6 predator species

Polar Park was founded by a Bardu forester and a retired zoologist who thought Norwegian zoos were embarrassing. They pooled land, bought fencing, and opened in 1994 with two wolves and a brown bear. The predator walk concept — a keeper takes small groups into the enclosure and the wolves come to be scratched — was pioneered here in 2004 and is now copied worldwide.

Ask about the wolves' names and rescue stories at the entrance kiosk — every one of them came from somewhere, and the keepers will tell you. The current alpha female was born here in 2019.

Setermoen · Norway's Army town

Garrison since 1898 · population 2,500, garrison 4,000

Setermoen has more soldiers than civilians. It is the largest inland garrison in Norway, home of the Northern Brigade, and the reason the E6 through here is as good as it is — the road has to move tanks. This is also why the local Coop Prix is open until 22:00 on Saturdays when everything else in northern Norway shuts at 18:00.

Kristian Birkeland & the aurora

1908 Terrella experiment · aurora finally explained

The Norwegian physicist Kristian Birkeland was the first person to work out what the aurora actually is. He built a magnetised sphere in a vacuum chamber, fired an electron beam at it, and produced auroras on a laboratory ball in Oslo — proving in 1908 that they were charged particles from the sun colliding with Earth's magnetic field. Every field guide to the northern lights still uses his diagrams.

Notice the 200-krone note in your wallet — that is Birkeland on the front, and his Terrella apparatus in the security strip.

Told to the little one · The wolf and the moon

A story to save for the drive

There is an old Sámi story about a wolf who loved the moon so much he tried to eat it — and every full moon since, he opens his mouth as wide as he can and swallows just a little bit of the light, which is why the moon gets smaller and smaller until it is dark. But every time it is dark, another wolf feels sorry for the moon and pushes it back up bit by bit, until it is full again. Then the first wolf gets hungry, and it starts over.
